Getting started

The autonomus time calculator is designed to use real world data and variables to provide semi-accurate (Hopefully becoming more accurate) time for autonomus paths

Configeration

  • Create a path on the custom pather, than copy and paste into the box
  • Add your robots weight, ideally after taking a real measurement
  • Add your wheel size and rpm
  • Add the number of 11 watt and 5.5 watt motors on your drive train
  • Add the efficenty calculated based on the formula: output energy / input energy x 100% or (input energy - friction) / input energy x 100%
